Trainer rules

  • 1 Pokémon per human. Little trainers count too!
  • Only stickers with a number count.
  • Number already taken? The Pokédex will tell you. Pick another sticker.
  • No trading or swapping once you've checked in.
  • You must be at the party to win. If nobody claims it, we draw again.
  • Each Pokémon can only win once.
